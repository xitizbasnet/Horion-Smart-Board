# 📌 User Manual: Configuring the Camera on Horion Smart Board

---

# 1. Objective

This guide provides step-by-step instructions to configure smart camera settings on a Horion Smart Board.

The configuration helps optimize:

* Camera framing
* Video call experience
* Meeting room visibility
* Intelligent tracking behavior

---

# 2. Pre-requisites

Before starting, ensure the following:

* Horion Smart Board is properly connected to a power source
* User has physical access to the board
* A stylus or finger is available for navigation

---

# 3. Step-by-Step Instructions

---

## Step 1: Power On the Device

* Press the power button on the Horion Smart Board
* Wait for the system to fully boot and display the dashboard

---

## Step 2: Open Right-Side Navigation Panel

* Locate the right-side navigation bar
* If not visible, swipe from the right edge of the screen to the left

---

## Step 3: Explore Available Options

The right-side panel includes:

* Home
* Paint
* Side Arrow (expand more options)

---

## Step 4: Access Settings Menu

* Tap the **Side Arrow icon** to expand options
* Tap **Settings**

> Note: Additional options become available after expansion.

---

## Step 5: Open Left Navigation Menu

Inside Settings:

* A left-side navigation panel will appear
* Scroll through available configuration options

---

## Step 6: Go to Accessibility Settings

* Tap **Accessibility**

This section includes usability and camera-related features.

---

## Step 7: Configure Smart Camera Settings

Under **Accessibility → Smart Camera**, available options include:

* Speaker Mode
* Ordinary Preview
* Auto Framing
* EPTZ (Electronic Pan-Tilt-Zoom)

---

# 3.1 What is the Smart Camera in Horion?

The Smart Camera in Horion panels is a built-in or connected camera system that can:

* Automatically manage video framing
* Perform zooming and panning
* Track speakers during meetings
* Frame participants or groups intelligently

It is designed to enhance video conferencing and classroom experiences.

---

# 3.2 Smart Camera Modes Explained

---

## 3.2.1 EPTZ (Electronic Pan-Tilt-Zoom) — Recommended Mode

### What it does

* Simulates camera movement electronically (pan, tilt, zoom)
* Uses software-based adjustment instead of physical movement
* Provides manually controlled framing

### Why use it

* No mechanical parts → less wear and tear
* Faster and stable operation
* Ideal for small to medium rooms
* Provides professional and stable framing

> 📌 Best suited for structured meetings and presentations

---

## 3.2.2 Speaker Mode (Not Recommended Unless Needed)

### What it does

* Tracks active speaker automatically
* Zooms in on the person speaking
* Switches focus between speakers

### Drawbacks

* Can be distracting in group discussions
* Frequent switching reduces stability
* Not suitable for collaborative environments

---

## 3.2.3 Ordinary Preview

### What it does

* Displays a fixed camera view
* No tracking or zoom features

### Use Case

* Static monitoring
* Basic video display needs

### Limitations

* No intelligent framing
* Less professional appearance

---

## 3.2.4 Auto Framing

### What it does

* Detects all participants in the room
* Automatically adjusts zoom and framing

### Drawbacks

* May behave inconsistently with movement
* Can struggle in poor lighting conditions
* May not maintain stable framing

---

# 3.3 Why Choose EPTZ?

EPTZ is recommended because it provides:

* Manually controlled professional framing
* Smooth and non-distracting video behavior
* Reduced dependency on hardware movement
* Faster and more reliable performance

---

# 3.4 Summary

| Mode             | Purpose                       | Recommendation   | Notes                        |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------- |
| EPTZ             | Electronic controlled framing | ✅ Recommended    | Best for meetings/classrooms |
| Speaker Mode     | Focus on active speaker       | ❌ Limited use    | Can be distracting           |
| Ordinary Preview | Static view                   | ❌ Basic use only | No smart features            |
| Auto Framing     | Auto participant framing      | ❌ Situational    | May be unstable              |

> 📌 For meeting rooms, classrooms, and online teaching setups, **EPTZ is the preferred mode**.

---

## Step 8: Finalize Setup

* Select **EPTZ mode**
* Settings apply automatically
* Smart camera is now ready for use

---

# 4. Result after EPTZ Selection

The Horion Smart Board camera is successfully configured to **EPTZ mode**, providing:

* Better framing stability
* Improved meeting visibility
* Professional video output
* Smooth camera behavior

---

# 5. Notes

* Camera settings can be modified anytime via:
  **Settings → Accessibility → Smart Camera**
* Ensure proper lighting for optimal camera performance
* Recommended mode for most environments: **EPTZ**
